# 05 · Kernel Foundry

**Current leading direction.** An underground machine dungeon with compact dispatch controls and a vertical descent toward the Kernel. Keep the existing brown-and-gold palette and Shardrun's card/program interface. The simple drawings establish composition; the next art pass should replace them with richer stylized game art.

## Navigation and spacing

Play and Academy are small launch actions on the left. Library, Character and Settings use a low utility dock. Opening a section reveals one small working page over the same underground scene; Back or Escape returns to the opener. The selected destination remains visible. Plain menu pages take roughly 15–25% of the canvas; controls are approximately 40–48 pixels tall with readable text. Working screens have more content space.

The map descends from upper chambers to lower foundations. Amber marks the travelled route and current position; shapes and text identify room types. A compact encounter note appears beside the route. Deck and stats open on demand, leaving the route visible.

The battle retains the top run status, hero on the left, foes on the right, a program-slot row, the hand beneath it, draw/hold/discard, and a code/work pane. Current shard artwork and fonts are used. Purple is a restrained shard accent, while the environment and ordinary menu surfaces stay brown and amber.

## Character → Skins

**Character** replaces Wardrobe in this proposal. Choose a skin, inspect the same actor used in battle, then select **Use this skin**. The appearance persists for the local profile across menus and runs and does not change combat power. The preview demonstrates persistence in browser storage only; the actual rename and game persistence are deferred to implementation.

## Art direction

Brown stone, brass mechanisms, lantern light, deep shafts and roots/cables that lead down. Richer painted or cel-shaded art should preserve broad shapes and readable staging. Character detail should remain suitable for normal gameplay scale. See the [next art brief](ART_BRIEF.md).
