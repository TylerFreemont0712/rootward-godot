# Image generation prompt set

Tool: ChatGPT built-in image generation. Use case: `stylized-concept`. The existing Rootward Emberfox concept was used as the style reference for the first full-front design. All subsequent sheets used `reference/full-front.png` as their character reference; accessory sheets also used the side and back views.

## Canonical design

Draw one adult female Rootward playable skin, Vesper the Star-Script Witch. Give her a large soft floppy midnight-blue pointed witch hat with a brass crescent, short purple hair, a short plum mantle ending above the elbows, a teal tunic with one large gold star-rune, navy shorts, dark tights, crescent-cuff boots, fingerless gloves, and a closed spellbook fixed at her left hip. Use Emberfox-compatible anime adventure-game linework: bold contours, flat two-tone cel shading, broad readable colors, around six heads tall. Full-body front orthographic relaxed A-pose, empty visible hands and separated limbs, complete hat and boots on plain white. No integrated spell effects, extra characters, labels, or text.

## Alternate anime level

Using the canonical image as identity reference, redraw the same costume and pose with slightly larger expressive eyes and head, roughly 5.5 heads tall, heavier outer ink line, chunkier shapes, and simpler flat shading. Preserve the same colors and accessories.

## Character views

- `full-left-profile.png`: Same costume and proportions, strict orthographic left profile, nose to image right. Show hat brim thickness, mantle depth, tunic layers, book at her left hip, boot heel.
- `full-back.png`: Same costume and proportions, strict orthographic back. Show short purple bob under hat, back of plum mantle and teal tunic, book on her left hip. Do not repeat the chest star on the back.
- `full-three-quarter.png`: Same costume and neutral pose at approximately 45 degrees, revealing brim thickness, mantle depth, and book attachment.
- `head-front-left-profile.png`: Two matched large head busts, strict front and left profile, without hat or mantle, same purple bob and face, neutral expression.

## Body blockout

Draw the same character's proportions as a plain, non-anatomical articulated gray artist's mannequin, with a blank head and short purple hair cap. No costume or props. Use geometric torso and pelvis masses, cylinder limbs, and visible ball joints. One full-body orthographic front sheet and one left-profile sheet, neutral A-pose, white background. These are joint-placement guides, not anatomy or texture sources.

## Accessory sheets

- `hat-turnaround.png`: Empty standalone hat in matched front, side, and back views; midnight-blue soft cone and wide brim, burgundy underside, brass crescent, small dangling gold star.
- `mantle-front-back.png`: Empty plum and gold short mantle in front and back views, ending above elbows, clasp at chest, no character.
- `spellbook-turnaround.png`: Closed navy hip grimoire in front, spine, and back views, one large gold star, brass corners, ivory pages, belt attachment.
- `boots-gloves.png`: Front and side of the brown crescent-cuff boot; back and palm of the navy fingerless glove; simple gold trim, no character.

All sheets: isolated on white, clean anime linework, flat cel shading, no scenery, labels, text, or watermark. Front design is canonical if generated sheets differ.
