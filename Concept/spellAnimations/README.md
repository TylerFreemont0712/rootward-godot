# Spell animation concepts

These are VFX exploration boards on black backgrounds. Each board has several distinct options arranged for side-by-side review; the black background is a concept backdrop, not a transparency channel or finished sprite sheet.

- [Elemental bolts](01-elemental-bolts.png): projectile silhouettes and trails.
- [Wards and barriers](02-wards-and-barriers.png): shields, gates, mirrors, and a root ward.
- [Radial glyph bursts](03-radial-glyph-bursts.png): round, glyph-like energy shapes.
- [Impacts and bursts](04-impacts-and-bursts.png): hit sparks and elemental impacts.
- [Roots and ultimates](05-roots-and-ultimates.png): larger signature effects.

The initial concept run also planned a dedicated casting-circle board. The built-in image generator hit its team usage limit before that board could be made.
