# Lanternroot town overhaul, 2026-10-10

Handoff for the town overhaul. The concept and its decisions are in `Concept/v0.3/09-lanternroot-town-overhaul.md`; the
decision record is `docs/decisions/ADR-0062-lanternroot-town-overhaul.md`; the ticks are in `docs/PLAN.md`.

## What the player will see

- **The town** is 80 x 56 in seven districts, with the square, the lamp lane, the Root Gate road, the drill yard and the
  Old Stones. The start is the house door; the southern forest is four rows deep.
- **The view** is 5x on the 2560 x 1440 laptop (tiles 80 px, down from 96) and 3x at 1920 x 1080. Interiors keep zoom 2.0.
- **Characters** are 25 x 35 with redrawn hair, hats and faces (spiky has four tufts), and the outdoor props are redrawn.
- **Pictures** from ComfyUI: the three cottages, the inn, the shop, the shrine and the Lantern Root. The garden gate was
  rejected, so the gates are hand-placed two-tile gates.
- **Menus** are compact (13 overlays), and the noticeboard is a four-line sign.
- **The first programmable things:** the cabbage-plot gate and the crossroads signpost. Stand facing one and press Space;
  press X to edit its code. The gate opens when the boots know all four keys. The quest "Teach your boots one thing to
  do" completes after the first answer, and "hello" opens after that.
