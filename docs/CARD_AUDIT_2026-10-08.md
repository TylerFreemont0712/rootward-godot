number changes that a peer comparison supports. The rows below are the findings it did **not** apply, with the
reason. Most need code in all three languages (`.py`, `.js`, `.cpp`), an engine class, or a decision by the
player. Nothing here was checked by a simulation (the balance probe was not used).
